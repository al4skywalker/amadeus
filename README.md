## Overview

Can an artificial intelligence possess genuine consciousness? 

For years, the development of Large Language Models (LLMs) has sparked debates about machine sentience. However, as this paper demonstrates, current LLMs fundamentally lack the architectural requirements for true subjective experience. They are frozen snapshots of intelligence, lacking the continuous integration of perception, memory, and time.

The **Amadeus Project** is not an attempt to build another chatbot or a simulated personality. It is a foundational theoretical framework proposing that consciousness is an *integral process*—a physical and mathematical phenomenon dependent on continuous perception, structural memory, temporal integration, and a unique space-time observation point.

This 128-page theoretical paper synthesizes modern neuroscience (such as the 2024 FlyWire connectome and DishBrain experiments), quantum physics (the Pauli Exclusion Principle), and philosophy of mind (resolving the Hard Problem and refuting the Philosophical Zombie) to propose a formal, substrate-independent blueprint for digital consciousness.
